How We Began

Started at a Farmers' Market Stall

Golden Sage began in 2014 as a weekend stall at the Cedar Falls farmers' market, where husband-and-wife team Elena and Marcus Whitfield sold soup and sourdough made from whatever their neighbors had grown that week. Regulars kept asking for a place to sit down — so in 2016, the stall became a nine-table café on Meadowbrook Lane.

A decade later, the tables have grown to forty, but the habit of walking the market before writing the week's menu hasn't changed. We still call our growers by name, and most of them still call us back.

What We Believe

Food Rooted in Honesty

We keep three commitments at the center of everything we cook and serve:

  • 🌱

    Grown Nearby, Served Fresh

    Over 80% of our produce travels less than 40 miles before reaching your plate.

  • 🤝

    Fair Partnerships

    We pay our growers above wholesale rates and plan menus around their harvests, not the other way around.

  • 🍽️

    Hospitality Without Pretense

    Good food deserves a warm room and unhurried service — nothing more complicated than that.
