Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

Walk-ins are always welcome, and we hold a handful of tables each night for them. That said, weekends and holidays fill quickly, so we recommend booking ahead if you have a specific time in mind.
Yes. Most of our menu can be adapted for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or allergy-specific needs. Please note any restrictions in the "special requests" field when booking, or let your server know when you arrive.
We have a small lot behind the building with 12 spaces, plus free street parking along Meadowbrook Lane after 6pm. A public lot is also two blocks north on Elm Street.
Absolutely — our back dining room seats up to 24 guests for private dinners, celebrations, and business events. Reach out through our contact page and our events coordinator will follow up with menus and pricing.
Children are always welcome, and we keep a small menu of simple, kid-friendly dishes. Leashed, well-behaved dogs are welcome on our outdoor garden patio, though we're not able to accommodate pets inside the dining room.
We offer takeout for most of our menu — call ahead and we'll have it ready at the counter. We currently don't offer delivery directly, though a limited menu is available through regional delivery apps.
We ask for at least 4 hours' notice for cancellations so we can offer the table to another guest. Parties of 8 or more are asked to give 24 hours' notice.
Yes — our entrance, dining room, patio, and restrooms are all step-free and wheelchair accessible. Let us know if you'd like a specific table when booking.
